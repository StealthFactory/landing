See Max in action

Start with today

Capture a thought in Daily Notes. Use the calendar to get back to the conversation that started it.

Follow an idea across pages

Open a project’s backlinks to see where it came up. Embed a decision block wherever you need it again.

Keep your context open

Stack a brief and a research note in the right pane while you keep writing in the main pane.

Move ideas in blocks

Move a whole branch with its children. Select several to-dos and press ⌘Return to toggle them together.

Jump to the words you remember

Search across pages and blocks with ⌘U. Shift-click a result or press Shift-Return to open it on the right.

Focus on the sentence

Choose a block, paragraph, or sentence to keep in focus. The surrounding text fades as you write.

Keep code with your notes

Use Markdown tables, lists, and code fences. Tag a fence with a language to see syntax colors in preview and while editing.

Private by design.

Max works offline and keeps your vault on your Mac. Your notes, attachments, and search data are encrypted at rest.

Lock your vault when you’re done. Take your notes with you as readable Markdown or make an encrypted Max archive.
